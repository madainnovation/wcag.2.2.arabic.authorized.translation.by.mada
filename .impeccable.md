## Design Context

### Users

Arabic readers of WCAG 2.2 and the team reviewing the translation against the
English Recommendation. The user requested full English–Arabic comparison text.

### Brand Personality

Use the original W3C English document as the visual reference, as explicitly
requested by the user: formal, legible, restrained.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the W3C document structure, typography hierarchy, blue headings, notes,
and contents list. Adapt direction and spacing for Arabic. This is a standards
document, not a new brand or product design. Support the supplied 1159px view
and narrower windows without horizontal document scrolling.

### Design Principles

- Preserve all normative content and the W3C section order.
- Keep decimal criterion numbers in left-to-right order within Arabic text.
- Use logical spacing and normal flow for RTL lists; avoid negative offsets.
- Keep authoring methods, AI/process explanations, and validation logs outside
  the reading document, as requested in the browser annotation.
- Preserve factual translation credits and a concise draft status.
